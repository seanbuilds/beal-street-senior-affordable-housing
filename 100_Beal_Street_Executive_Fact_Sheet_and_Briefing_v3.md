## 1. Executive Summary & Core Project Parameters

The **100 Beal Street Senior Affordable Housing Project** is a proposed 3-story, 68-unit affordable senior apartment community situated on an 8.6-acre portion (**Lot B**) of the 15.014-acre parcel owned in fee simple by the **Hingham Housing Authority (HHA)**. Located directly adjacent to **Bare Cove Park** and draining toward the **Weymouth Back River Area of Critical Environmental Concern (ACEC)**, the development represents an independent public housing initiative advancing toward a negotiated, friendly **Chapter 40B Comprehensive Permit** before the Hingham Zoning Board of Appeals (ZBA).

### 2.1 Resolution of the 1989 Deed Reverter & 2019 Select Board Vote
The 1989 deed from the Town to HHA contained a 30-year conditional reverter ([Book 09097, Page 160](file:///Volumes/BLK4TB/Housing%20Topics/Beal%20Street%20Senior%20Affordable%20Housing/documents/Hingham_Beal_Street_Deed_1989_and_ZBA_Decision_v1.md#L108-L134)):
* Under Massachusetts General Laws (M.G.L. c. 184A, § 3 and c. 184, § 23), any right of entry or possibility of reverter terminates automatically after **thirty years** from the date of the instrument if no breach occurs.
* The deed was executed on March 7, 1989. The 30-year limitation expired on **March 7, 2019**.
* On **February 26, 2019**—exactly nine days before the expiration date—the Hingham Board of Selectmen convened in executive session with Real Estate Counsel Susan Murphy and voted 3–0 to authorize litigation against HHA to enforce a 2001 MOU.
* Under threat of litigation, the prior HHA Board voted on June 5, 2019 to convey 19 acres to the Town.
* However, the **Massachusetts Department of Housing and Community Development (DHCD)** intervened, issuing legal warnings that local housing authorities cannot alienate public housing property without state approval (M.G.L. c. 121B, § 34), and recorded the restriction at **Book 51379, Page 244** on July 18, 2019.

### 2.2 The Rescission Vote of September 7, 2021
On **September 7, 2021**, under Executive Director **James Marathas**, the HHA Board of Commissioners voted **4–0 to formally rescind the June 5, 2019 transfer motion**:
> *"Commissioner O’Meara made a motion, seconded by Commissioner Lauter, to rescind a prior Motion made at the June 5, 2019 Special Meeting... The Motion approved was to Transfer a parcel Land comprising approximately nineteen (19) acres to the Town of Hingham... That Motion is now rescinded and the land in question shall remain in the ownership of the Hingham Housing Authority. The Motion was passed on a 4–0 vote."*

This vote extinguished the inter-municipal dispute and confirmed HHA's fee simple title.

## 5. Zoning, Chapter 40B Permitting & Safe Harbor Dynamics

* **Official & Open Space (OO) Zoning:** Multi-family residential housing is barred as of right in OO zoning districts, necessitating a Comprehensive Permit under **M.G.L. c. 40B** before the Hingham Zoning Board of Appeals (ZBA).
* **Safe Harbor Benchmark (760 CMR 56.03):** Because Hingham's Subsidized Housing Inventory (SHI) exceeds 10%, the Town has statutory safe harbor. The ZBA has legal latitude to condition or reject hostile 40B petitions. Therefore, 100 Beal Street is proceeding as a **negotiated "friendly" 40B** with broad municipal alignment.
* **Abutter Challenge:** Craftsman Village abutters (led by Shannon Brinkley) have urged the Town to invoke its Safe Harbor leverage to scale the project down from Peabody's 68 units to the 46-unit concept originally submitted by competing bidder AHSC.
