# 100 Beal Street: The 37-Year Journey
## From Naval Munitions Bunkers to State-Protected Senior Affordable Housing (1989–2026)

**A Comprehensive Documentary Review of Public Deeds, Town Meeting Votes & Title History**

* **Location:** 100 Beal Street, Hingham, MA (School Tract II)
* **Record Property Owner:** Hingham Housing Authority (Independent State Entity, M.G.L. c. 121B)
* **Proposed Development:** 68-Unit Age-Restricted Senior Affordable Community (62+)
* **Selected Developer:** Peabody Properties / AHSC (99-Year Ground Lease)
* **Taxpayer Financial Impact:** Exactly $0 in Town Borrowing or Municipal Debt

---

# April 1989: The Deed & The 30-Year Reverter
## Town Conveys 15.014 Acres of Surplus Military Land to HHA

* **Execution Date:** March 7, 1989 | **Recording Date:** April 21, 1989
* **Registry Citation:** Plymouth County Registry of Deeds, **Book 09097, Page 158** (Instrument #29758)
* **Grantor:** Inhabitants of the Town of Hingham (pursuant to 1987 ATM Article 53)
* **Grantee:** Hingham Housing Authority | **Consideration:** $45,751.00
* **Property Scope:** Entire 15.014 Net Acres (School Tract II)
* **Use Restriction:** Property to be used *solely* for a residential educational facility for emotionally disturbed adolescents
* **The 30-Year Reverter (Page 160):** Re-entry right *"shall terminate after thirty years from the date hereof as provided in M.G.L. c. 184A, § 3"*
* **Statutory Expiration Date:** March 7, 2019 (Exactly 30 calendar years)

---

# 1990 to Present: Continuous Public Service
## The 12-Bed Adolescent Facility Operates Without Interruption

* **Facility Footprint:** 12-bed group home constructed on dedicated 2.0-acre parcel fronting Beal Street
* **Funding Program:** Commonwealth Chapter 689 specialized public housing funds
* **Operational Oversight:** Licensed providers under continuous contracts with Department of Children and Families (DCF)
* **Unbroken Operation:** Over 35 consecutive years of continuous, safe public service
* **Condition Precedent:** Town re-entry required cessation of adolescent care
* **Zero Abandonment:** At no point was the adolescent facility closed or the mission abandoned
* **Active Status Today:** Youth home operates undisturbed on Lot 1, completely separate from proposed senior housing

---

# June 2001: Demolition, Remediation & Division
## WWII Munitions Bunkers Cleared; 8-Acre Surplus Plateau Created

* **Naval History:** Interior 13 acres held abandoned reinforced concrete naval ammunition bunkers
* **Remediation Funding:** Town funded demolition of structures and environmental soil cleanup
* **Administrative MOU:** Town and HHA executed Memorandum of Understanding to coordinate future surplus land use
* **Plan 100 of 2001:** Gale Associates division survey recorded in Plymouth Plan Book 44, Page 412
* **Lot 1 (2.000 Acres):** Active adolescent group home with dedicated Beal Street frontage
* **Lot 2 (8.020 Acres):** Remediated, buildable upland plateau with direct Beal Street access
* **Milestone Result:** Established clean, separate building lot for future public housing needs

---

# May 2006: Town Meeting Mandates Senior Housing
## Declared 2/3 Supermajority Authorizes Deed Release for Affordable Housing

* **Legislative Assembly:** Hingham Annual Town Meeting (May 1, 2006)
* **Warrant Article 38:** Sponsored to unlock Lot 2 surplus plateau for senior housing
* **Voting Threshold:** <span class="highlight">Declared 2/3 supermajority</span> approved by Hingham voters; attested by Town Clerk Eileen McCracken
* **Enacted Text:** Authorized Selectmen to amend 1989 deed restriction on School Tract II to add senior affordable housing
* **The Democratic Will:** Clear town-wide mandate dedicating the plateau to senior community living
* **The Administrative Breakdown:** Town Hall never drafted, executed, or recorded that deed release at the Registry
* **The Resulting Title Cloud:** 1989 adolescent restriction remained technically unreleased on public land records

---

# 2017–2018: The Unexecuted Deed Resurfaces
## Beal Street Due Diligence Reveals the Unrecorded Release

* **Title Rediscovery:** Town Counsel Susan Murphy discovers 2006 deed release was never recorded
* **Selectmen Meeting Jan 30, 2018:** Selectman Karen Johnson details 2001 bunker remediation history
* **Selectmen Meeting Feb 13, 2018:** HHA Chair Bob Keys requests review of restrictions and covenants
* **Town Position:** Select Board argues that because back 13 acres were never used for youth, land should belong to Town
* **Corporate Independence:** HHA is an independent public body corporate under M.G.L. c. 121B
* **Statutory Mission:** HHA commissioners decline voluntary surrender of public housing land
* **Approaching Deadline:** 30-year deed reverter set to expire on March 7, 2019

---

# February 2019: The 30-Year Deadline & Lawsuit Vote
## Select Board Votes 3–0 to Sue Housing Authority Nine Days Before Deadline

* **Statutory Countdown:** March 7, 2019 (Exactly 30 years from March 7, 1989 deed execution)
* **Legal Advice:** Town Counsel advises that Town's right of re-entry terminates forever under c. 184A, § 3
* **Executive Session:** February 26, 2019 at 6:45 PM (Nine days before statutory expiration)
* **Open Session Action:** Select Board reconvenes at 7:00 PM (Chairman Paul Healey presiding)
* **The Vote:** Chairman Healey moves, Selectman Johnson seconds; <span class="highlight">voted 3–0 unanimous</span>
* **Official Mandate:** *"To file litigation regarding enforcement of agreement as to 100 Beal Street"*
* **Core Objective:** Force HHA to deed 100 Beal Street back to Town Hall before statutory reverter lapsed

---

# June 2019: Emergency Transfer Vote Under Duress
## Facing Litigation, Housing Authority Board Votes to Surrender 19 Acres

* **Meeting Date:** June 5, 2019 (Special Meeting of HHA Board of Commissioners)
* **Litigation Threat:** Board confronted with imminent court filing by Town of Hingham
* **The Motion:** Board votes to approve transfer of ~19 acres off Beal Street to municipal control
* **Scope of Surrender:** Contemplated relinquishing Lot 2 and associated Housing Authority parcels
* **The Illusion of Finality:** Town Hall believed the property dispute had been settled
* **Statutory Constraints:** Local housing authorities cannot convey public housing property unilaterally
* **State Financial Interest:** Chapter 689 funding triggered mandatory Commonwealth supervision
* **Imminent State Reaction:** State regulatory authorities intervene to block the transfer

---

# Summer 2019: The Commonwealth Steps In
## DHCD Warns Public Housing Land Cannot Be Conveyed Under M.G.L. c. 121B, § 34

* **Governing Authority:** Massachusetts General Laws Chapter 121B (Housing and Urban Renewal)
* **Legal Status:** Housing authorities are independent state public corporations, not Town departments
* **Municipal Separation:** Board of Selectmen has zero statutory authority to seize or command housing assets
* **Supervisory Regulator:** Massachusetts DHCD (Associate Director Amy Stitely)
* **Statutory Bar:** M.G.L. c. 121B, § 34 strictly forbids transferring public housing land without written state approval
* **Regulatory Warning Letters:** Issued April 19 & June 26, 2019 to HHA and Town Counsel
* **State Decision:** DHCD formally refuses to consent to municipal conveyance

---

# July 2019: The Legal Lock on Title
## Commonwealth Records Notice of Statutory Transfer Restriction

* **Recording Timestamp:** July 18, 2019 at 1:44 PM
* **Registry Citation:** Plymouth County Registry of Deeds, **Book 51379, Page 244** (Doc #55967)
* **Grantor/Regulator:** Commonwealth of Massachusetts DHCD
* **Encumbered Real Estate:** Assessor Map 58, Block 0, Lot 23 (100 Beal Street)
* **Operational Effect:** Public notice to all title examiners that Chapter 121B, § 34 encumbers title
* **Legal Nullity:** Any deed executed without DHCD written approval is void as a matter of state law
* **ALTA Survey Note:** Explicitly recognized as Schedule B, Item 5 encumbrance on 2024 survey
* **Permanent Protection:** Land permanently protected for public affordable housing

---

# September 2021: HHA Rescission & The Unresolved Title Cloud
## Ownership Reaffirmed, But 1989 Adolescent Restriction Remains on Record

* **Assembly:** September 7, 2021 Regular Meeting at 30 Thaxter Street
* **The Motion:** Moved by Commissioner O'Meara, seconded by Commissioner Lauter
* **Roll Call Vote:** Commissioners Suchecki, O'Meara, Lauter, Buhr vote <span class="highlight">4–0 unanimous</span>
* **Minute Ruling:** *"That Motion is now rescinded and the land shall remain in the ownership of the HHA"*
* **Ownership Retained:** Fee title remained with HHA under state statutory protection
* **The Continuing Title Cloud:** The 2021 vote did NOT erase the 1989 deed restriction from Registry
* **Missing Release:** Town Hall never recorded the 2006 deed release on public land records
* **2024 ALTA Survey Note 3:** Control Point Associates / Kellem & Kellem title abstract explicitly states:  
  *"THEREFORE NEED RELEASE OR CHANGE OF THIS RESTRICTION FROM THE TOWN OF HINGHAM."*

# August 2025: Peabody Award (Conditional Ground Lease)
## 68 Senior Units Under a 99-Year Ground Lease Contingent on Title Resolution

* **Competitive Procurement:** 155-Page RFP issued April 16, 2025 under M.G.L. c. 30B / c. 121B
* **Selection Vote:** August 12, 2025 HHA meeting; passed <span class="highlight">4–0 unanimous</span>
* **Designated Developer:** Peabody Properties / Affordable Housing Services Corp. (AHSC)
* **Development Scope:** 68 One- and Two-Bedroom Rental Apartments for Seniors 62 and older
* **99-Year Ground Lease:** Land ownership remains permanently with Hingham Housing Authority
* **Upfront Consideration:** Developer pays $500,000 upfront lease fee to Housing Authority
* **Contingent Execution:** Ground lease closing requires state approvals and formally clearing the deed restriction
* **Taxpayer Obligation:** Exactly $0 in Town borrowing or municipal taxpayer debt
