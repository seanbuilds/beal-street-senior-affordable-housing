1990 to Present: Continuous Public Service

The 12-Bed Adolescent Facility Operates Without Interruption

Historical Context & Authority

  • Facility Footprint: 12-bed group home constructed on dedicated 2.0-acre parcel fronting Beal Street
  • Funding Program: Commonwealth Chapter 689 specialized public housing funds
  • Operational Oversight: State youth services contract and licensed child care provider
  • Continuous Public Service: Over 35 consecutive years of continuous operation

Documented Legal Findings

  • Legal Standard: Re-entry required cessation of adolescent residential use
  • Zero Default: Facility never ceased operations or abandoned its mission
  • State Encumbrance: Chapter 689 funding attached permanent state public housing covenants
  • Unclouded Title on Lot 1: Active youth home remains undisturbed on its 2-acre lot
