## 1. Executive Summary & Verification Purpose

The 37-year history of 100 Beal Street involves interlocking jurisdictions: the **Town of Hingham** (municipal grantor), the **Hingham Housing Authority (HHA)** (independent state-chartered owner), the **Commonwealth of Massachusetts** (EOHLC / DHCD regulatory supervisor), the **Massachusetts Department of Environmental Protection (MassDEP)**, and private developer **Peabody Properties**.

While the core documentary foundation is firmly established through recorded deeds, Town Meeting warrants, and certified board minutes, a thorough title and administrative audit requires verifying several referenced agreements, administrative letters, and pending contract executions. 

This guide serves two distinct functions:
1. **The Evidentiary Ledger:** Catalogs all primary records currently possessed, inspected, and verified in the project repository.
2. **The Actionable Verification Checklist:** Outlines the remaining records that require physical or online inspection, explaining why each matters, where it is held, and how to obtain it.

## 3. The 7 Critical Open Records Requiring Verification

The following seven records represent the remaining unobtained documents, uninspected dockets, or pending contract executions:

### Item 1: Complete Grantor/Grantee Registry Run (2006–Present)
* **Official Custodian:** Plymouth County Registry of Deeds (Brockton, MA / Online Portal).
* **The Core Question:** Did the Hingham Board of Selectmen **ever** execute and record a formal release, amendment, or deed modification pursuant to 2006 Annual Town Meeting Article 38?
* **Why It Matters:** The 2024 ALTA Survey Note 3 indicates no release was recorded, leaving the 1989 adolescent restriction encumbering title. A comprehensive search across grantor and grantee indexes confirms conclusively whether any instrument was recorded under an alternate spelling, document code, or parcel identifier.
* **Verification Protocol:**
  1. Access `https://www.plymouthdeeds.org/` (Search Records).
  2. Search **Grantor:** `Town of Hingham` &rarr; **Grantee:** `Hingham Housing Authority` (Date range: `05/01/2006` to `Present`).
  3. Filter/Review Document Types: `REL` (Release), `AMD` (Amendment), `MOD` (Modification), `AGR` (Agreement), `DEED` (Deed).
  4. Perform reverse search: **Grantor:** `Hingham Housing Authority` &rarr; **Grantee:** `Town of Hingham`.
* **Target Finding:** Absence of any recorded release confirms title remains encumbered by the 1989 deed condition, requiring Peabody and HHA to either secure an affirmative municipal release or extinguish/override local restrictions through a Chapter 40B Comprehensive Permit.

### Item 2: The Executed 2001 Memorandum of Understanding (MOU)
* **Official Custodian:** Hingham Town Clerk / Select Board Office / HHA Administration.
* **The Core Question:** What are the exact terms, covenants, expiration dates, and municipal remedies contained in the signed agreement dated on or about June 12, 2001?
* **Why It Matters:** Plan 100 of 2001 explicitly cites an agreement dated June 12, 2001. In 2019, the Select Board cited taxpayer funds expended under this agreement to justify their 3–0 litigation vote. Without the executed text, the legal validity of the Town’s contractual claims remains unverified.
* **Verification Protocol:**
  1. File a Massachusetts Public Records Request (M.G.L. c. 66, § 10) with the Hingham Town Clerk and Select Board.
  2. Request: *"The fully executed Memorandum of Understanding between the Town of Hingham and the Hingham Housing Authority dated on or about June 12, 2001 concerning School Tract II / 100 Beal Street."*
* **Target Finding:** Inspection of contract clauses to determine whether the MOU contained an expiration date, a re-conveyance obligation, or merely an administrative cost-sharing protocol.

### Item 3: MassCourts Litigation Docket Search (2019 Lawsuit Check)
* **Official Custodian:** Massachusetts Trial Court (Plymouth Superior Court & Massachusetts Land Court).
* **The Core Question:** Did Town Counsel (Susan Murphy / KP Law) actually file a formal civil complaint against HHA after the Select Board's 3–0 vote on February 26, 2019?
* **Why It Matters:** Establishes whether the 2019 conflict escalated into an active judicial proceeding or whether it was resolved entirely through administrative maneuvers prior to service of process.
* **Verification Protocol:**
  1. Access `https://www.masscourts.org/` (Public Case Search).
  2. Select Court Departments:
     - `Superior Court` &rarr; `Plymouth County Superior Court`
     - `Land Court` &rarr; `Land Court Department`
  3. Search Party Names:
     - Party 1: `Town of Hingham` (Plaintiff)
     - Party 2: `Hingham Housing Authority` (Defendant)
     - Filing Years: `2018` through `2022`
* **Target Finding:** If no docket exists, the dispute was resolved pre-litigation. If a docket exists, obtain the docket sheet and complaint to inspect the Town's formal claims and the case disposition (e.g., dismissal without prejudice).

### Item 4: State DHCD Warning Letters (April 19 & June 26, 2019)
* **Official Custodian:** Executive Office of Housing and Livable Communities (EOHLC, formerly DHCD).
* **The Core Question:** What specific statutory findings did Associate Director Amy Stitely issue regarding the illegality of transferring state-assisted Chapter 689 public housing land?
* **Why It Matters:** These letters are cited as the legal turning point in HHA minutes, prompting the state to record Book 51379, Page 244 and compelling HHA to rescind its transfer motion.
* **Verification Protocol:**
  1. Submit an online Public Records Request to the EOHLC Public Records Officer.
  2. Request: *"Copies of all letters and written guidance sent by Amy Stitely, Associate Director of the Division of Public Housing, to the Hingham Housing Authority, its Board of Commissioners, or Town of Hingham officials dated between April 1, 2019 and July 31, 2019 regarding 100 Beal Street (State Housing Project 689-01)."*
* **Target Finding:** Complete written analysis confirming Commonwealth supervisory jurisdiction under M.G.L. c. 121B, § 34.

### Item 5: Certified HHA Minutes & Votes (June 5, 2019 & August 25, 2026)
* **Official Custodian:** Hingham Housing Authority Administrative Office (100 Beal Street / 30 Thaxter Park).
* **The Core Question:**
  - What was the verbatim vote and attendance record at the June 5, 2019 Special Meeting where the board voted to transfer ~19 acres to the Town?
  - Did the Board vote on Item 9 of the August 25, 2026 Special Meeting agenda (approving the Land Disposition Agreement with AHSC Peabody Developers LLC), and what was the official vote tally?
* **Why It Matters:** An agenda establishes only what was scheduled. Approved, certified minutes are the sole legal evidence of official board action, vote tallies, and contract execution authorization.
* **Verification Protocol:**
  1. Request certified copies of approved minutes from the HHA Executive Director or Board Secretary.
  2. Inquire whether the August 25, 2026 minutes have been formally approved and signed.
* **Target Finding:** Confirms legal passage of Motion 9 and authorizes execution of the Land Disposition Agreement.

### Item 6: Executed Land Disposition Agreement (LDA) & Ground Lease
* **Official Custodian:** HHA Administration & Peabody Properties / AHSC.
* **The Core Question:** Has the Land Disposition and Development Agreement (LDDA) been signed by both parties, and what are the specific conditions precedent to closing?
* **Why It Matters:** The 155-page RFP package contained draft contract templates. The executed agreement establishes the binding milestone schedule, contingencies (deed release vs. 40B override, financing closing deadlines, DHCD Section 34 approval), and financial obligations.
* **Verification Protocol:**
  1. Request a copy of the executed LDDA from HHA.
  2. Monitor Plymouth County Registry of Deeds for subsequent recording of a *"Notice of Lease"* or *"Memorandum of Ground Lease"* upon financial closing.
* **Target Finding:** Confirms whether resolving the 1989 deed restriction is an explicit developer contingency before ground lease execution.

### Item 7: MassHousing Project Eligibility Letter (PEL) & Chapter 40B Filing
* **Official Custodian:** MassHousing (Subsidizing Agency) & Hingham Zoning Board of Appeals (ZBA).
* **The Core Question:** Has Peabody Properties submitted its application for Project Eligibility, has MassHousing issued a formal PEL under 760 CMR 56.00, and has a Comprehensive Permit application been submitted to the Hingham ZBA?
* **Why It Matters:** Under Massachusetts General Laws Chapter 40B, issuance of a Project Eligibility Letter is the mandatory prerequisite for local ZBA jurisdiction. The 40B Comprehensive Permit is also the legal vehicle that allows the ZBA to grant waivers from local bylaws and deed restrictions.
* **Verification Protocol:**
  1. Search MassHousing's 40B project directory for Hingham applications.
  2. Inspect the Hingham Zoning Board of Appeals public meeting portal for pending Chapter 40B filings for 100 Beal Street.
* **Target Finding:** Establishes the formal start of the local permitting review process and the statutory 180-day hearing window.
