## 1. Executive Summary & Core Project Parameters

The **100 Beal Street Senior Affordable Housing Project** is a proposed 3-story, 68-unit affordable senior apartment community situated on an 8.6-acre portion (**Lot B**) of the 15.014-acre parcel owned in fee simple by the **Hingham Housing Authority (HHA)**. Located directly adjacent to **Bare Cove Park** and draining toward the **Weymouth Back River Area of Critical Environmental Concern (ACEC)**, the development represents an independent public housing initiative advancing through a negotiated, friendly **Chapter 40B Comprehensive Permit** process before the Hingham Zoning Board of Appeals (ZBA).

### 2.2 Detailed History of the Center for Active Living (CAL)
1. **The Need (2011–2020):** Hingham’s existing senior center was established in 1997 in 5,200 sq. ft. of space on the lower level of Town Hall. With residents aged 60+ projected to reach 39% of Hingham’s population by 2035, UMass Gerontology Institute studies (*Aging in Hingham*, 2013 & 2022) documented that the Town Hall facility was severely undersized, had inadequate parking, and suffered from poor accessibility.
2. **Site Search (2020–2024):** After analyzing eight configurations at Town Hall and multiple town-owned sites, the Center for Active Living Building Committee (CALBC), assisted by EDM Studio, SLR Consulting, and Lifespan Design Studio, selected a parcel off **Bare Cove Park Drive**.
3. **Town Meeting 2025 (Article 15):** The Town voted to appropriate funds for design, architectural/engineering services, and Owner’s Project Manager (OPM) services to prepare bid documents for the Bare Cove Park Drive site. **Article 15 successfully passed.**
4. **Town Meeting April 2026 (Article 12):** The Select Board placed Article 12 on the warrant seeking authorization to borrow **$29,927,423** to construct the CAL facility.
   - **The Vote:** The article received **510 votes in favor and 470 votes opposed**.
   - **Outcome:** Although it achieved a majority (**52.0% Yes**), Massachusetts General Laws mandate a **two-thirds (2/3) supermajority (66.7%)** to approve municipal bond borrowing. Consequently, Article 12 **failed to pass**.
   - **False Narrative Dispelled:** Public debate over traffic and proximity to Bare Cove Park led some to conflate CAL with 100 Beal Street. However, Article 12 had zero connection to residential development, was never a "land swap," and its defeat had no legal effect on 100 Beal Street, where development rights had already been awarded to Peabody Properties in August 2025 under state procurement laws.

## 5. Legal Mechanics of the 99-Year Ground Lease

### 5.1 Contingent Commencement & Expiration Window
- **Commencement Mechanism:** The lease clock does not begin upon signing. Under Section 1.1 of the LDDA and Section 2.1 of the Ground Lease, the 99-year term triggers on the **"Commencement Date"**, defined as within 30 days following satisfaction of all Lease Contingencies.
- **Key Contingencies:** Issuance of an unappealable Chapter 40B Comprehensive Permit from the Hingham ZBA, execution of the EOHLC Regulatory Agreement, closing on all construction loans (LIHTC equity, state subsidy, private debt), and issuance of full building permits.
- **Projected Expiration:** With financial closing projected for **late 2027 to 2028**, the lease term will run 99 years through approximately **2126 to 2127**.

### 5.2 Absolute Triple-Net (NNN) Structure & Public Revenue
- **Zero Operating Liability:** The HHA has no obligation to maintain, repair, heat, insure, or operate the building.
- **Developer Responsibilities:** Peabody Properties is solely responsible for 100% of construction, capital repairs, utility bills, property insurance, and municipal assessments or Payments in Lieu of Taxes (PILOT).
- **Consideration:** Developer pays a minimum **$500,000 upfront lease payment** to the HHA at construction loan closing, alongside ongoing annual ground lease base rent.

### 5.3 100% Public Reversion (Section 15.1)
- During the 99-year term, Peabody Properties owns the physical improvements for tax credit (LIHTC) and depreciation purposes.
- Upon expiration of the 99th year (or earlier termination), full legal and physical title to the 3-story, 68-unit building, grounds, and infrastructure **automatically surrenders and reverts 100% to the Hingham Housing Authority** at zero public cost.

### 5.4 Exclusion of the Lot A Adolescent Facility
- The ground lease strictly demises **Lot B (~8.6 acres)**.
- **Lot A (~2.4 acres)**, containing the existing 12-bed special needs adolescent group home operated by Bay State Community Services, is explicitly excluded and remains under direct HHA ownership and operational jurisdiction.

## 6. Zoning, Chapter 40B Permitting & Safe Harbor Analysis

### 6.1 Official & Open Space District (OO) Constraints
- 100 Beal Street is situated within the **Official and Open Space District (OO)**.
- Multi-family housing is prohibited by right and cannot be permitted through standard municipal special permits. Consequently, a **Comprehensive Permit pursuant to M.G.L. c. 40B** before the Hingham ZBA is legally required.

### 6.2 Statutory Safe Harbor (760 CMR 56.03)
- Because Hingham’s Subsidized Housing Inventory (SHI) exceeds the statutory 10% threshold, the Town possesses statutory **safe harbor**. Under safe harbor, the ZBA has statutory authority to deny hostile 40B petitions without risk of being overridden by the state Housing Appeals Committee (HAC).
- **Strategic Reality:** 100 Beal Street is proceeding as a **negotiated "friendly" 40B**, relying on close municipal cooperation between the HHA, Peabody Properties, the Select Board, and the Conservation Commission.

## 7. Environmental Sensitivity: Bare Cove Park & Weymouth Back River ACEC

### 7.1 Watershed Adjacency & Drainage
- **Weymouth Back River ACEC:** Designated in 1982, the estuarine system possesses heightened ecological protection under 301 CMR 12.00.
- **Hockley Run & Tucker's Swamp:** Runoff and groundwater discharge from 100 Beal Street flow toward Hockley Run and Tucker's Swamp, tributaries directly feeding the Back River ACEC.
- **Stormwater Mandates:** The project must satisfy the highest standards of the MassDEP Stormwater Management Handbook (310 CMR 10.05(6)(k)), specifically controlling nitrogen loading, thermal discharge, and volume into Outstanding Resource Waters (ORW).

### 7.2 Binding Wetlands Envelope (MassDEP File No. 034-1509)
The November 7, 2024 Order of Resource Area Delineation (ORAD) establishes the unappealable wetland boundaries:
- Three Bordering Vegetated Wetland (BVW) systems (`WFA-1` to `WFA-100` and `WFB-1` to `WFB-23`).
- Two Isolated Vegetated Wetland (IVW) systems under local bylaw protection.
- A confirmed Vernal Pool in the southwestern quadrant, creating strict 100-foot buffer setbacks.
- 200-foot Riverfront Area associated with perennial tributaries.
