## 1. Executive Summary & Core Project Parameters

The **100 Beal Street Senior Affordable Housing Project** is a proposed 3-story, 68-unit affordable senior apartment community situated on an 8.6-acre portion (**Lot B**) of the 15.014-acre parcel owned in fee simple by the **Hingham Housing Authority (HHA)**. Located directly adjacent to **Bare Cove Park** and draining toward the **Weymouth Back River Area of Critical Environmental Concern (ACEC)**, the development represents a significant municipal affordable housing initiative advancing through a negotiated, friendly **Chapter 40B Comprehensive Permit** process before the Hingham Zoning Board of Appeals (ZBA).

## 4. Legal Mechanics of the 99-Year Ground Lease

### 4.1 Contingent Commencement & Expiration Timeframe
- **Contingent Clock:** Under Section 1.1 of the LDDA and Section 2.1 of the Ground Lease, the 99-year term does not commence on contract signing. It commences on the **"Commencement Date"**, legally defined as the date within 30 days after all financing and permitting contingencies are satisfied.
- **Lease Contingencies:** Include unappealable issuance of the Chapter 40B Comprehensive Permit, execution of the EOHLC Regulatory Agreement, closing of construction financing (LIHTC, state subsidies, private equity), and issuance of full building permits.
- **Projected Expiration:** With construction closing projected for **late 2027 to 2028**, the 99-year lease will expire approximately **2126 to 2127**.

### 4.2 Absolute Triple-Net (NNN) Financial Structure
- **Zero Municipal Burden:** The HHA has no obligation to maintain, repair, heat, insure, or operate the facility.
- **Tenant Obligations:** Peabody Properties (AHSC Developers LLC) is solely liable for 100% of capital construction, site civil infrastructure, routine and extraordinary structural maintenance, property insurance, utilities, and municipal taxes or Payments in Lieu of Taxes (PILOT).
- **Public Revenue:** The HHA receives a minimum **$500,000 upfront lease payment** at construction closing, supplemented by ongoing annual ground lease base rent.

### 4.3 100% Public Reversion (Section 15.1)
- During the 99-year term, Peabody Properties owns the building and physical improvements, entitling the developer to claim depreciation and Low-Income Housing Tax Credits (LIHTC).
- Upon the expiration of the 99th year (or earlier termination), Peabody must peaceably quit and surrender the premises in good condition. **Full 100% fee title to the 3-story, 68-unit building, common facilities, infrastructure, and grounds automatically reverts to the Hingham Housing Authority** at zero public acquisition cost.

### 4.4 Preservation of the Lot A Adolescent Facility
- The ground lease strictly covers **Lot B (~8.6 acres)**.
- **Lot A (~2.4 acres)**, containing the existing 12-bed special needs residential facility operated by Bay State Community Services, is explicitly carved out from the developer's leasehold and remains under direct HHA ownership and operational oversight.

## 5. Zoning, Chapter 40B Permitting & Safe Harbor Analysis

### 5.1 Official & Open Space District (OO) Constraints
- 100 Beal Street is situated within the Town of Hingham's **Official and Open Space District (OO)**.
- Under Section IV-A of the Hingham Zoning By-Law, multi-family residential development is prohibited by right and cannot be permitted through standard municipal special permits or site plan review.
- Consequently, a **Comprehensive Permit pursuant to M.G.L. c. 40B** before the Hingham Zoning Board of Appeals (ZBA) is the only legally viable path to construct the 68-unit residential community.

### 5.2 Statutory Safe Harbor (760 CMR 56.03)
- Under Massachusetts housing regulations, when a municipality’s Subsidized Housing Inventory (SHI) exceeds 10% of its year-round housing stock, the municipality achieves **statutory safe harbor**.
- Hingham’s SHI currently exceeds the 10% statutory threshold. Under safe harbor, a Zoning Board of Appeals has legal discretion to deny a hostile 40B application or impose local conditions without being overturned by the state Housing Appeals Committee (HAC).
- **Practical Implication:** The 100 Beal Street project cannot proceed as an adversarial 40B development. It is structured as a **negotiated "friendly" Chapter 40B**, requiring cooperative consensus between the HHA, Peabody Properties, the Select Board, the Conservation Commission, and the ZBA.

## 6. Environmental Sensitivity: Bare Cove Park & Weymouth Back River ACEC

### 6.1 Watershed Adjacency & Drainage
- **Weymouth Back River ACEC:** Designated in 1982 by the Commonwealth as an Area of Critical Environmental Concern, the Back River estuarine system possesses heightened ecological protection under 301 CMR 12.00.
- **Hockley Run & Tucker's Swamp:** Runoff and groundwater discharge from the eastern and southern sectors of 100 Beal Street flow toward Hockley Run and Tucker's Swamp, tributaries directly feeding the ACEC.
- **Stormwater Standards:** Stormwater management designs under the 40B permit must satisfy the highest standards of the MassDEP Stormwater Management Handbook (310 CMR 10.05(6)(k)), specifically mitigating nitrogen loading, thermal discharge, and peak volume run-off into Outstanding Resource Waters (ORW).

### 6.2 Binding Wetlands Envelope (MassDEP File No. 034-1509)
The November 7, 2024 Order of Resource Area Delineation (ORAD) establishes the unappealable wetland boundaries across the 15-acre tract:
1. **Three Bordering Vegetated Wetland (BVW) Systems:** Flags `WFA-1` to `WFA-100` and `WFB-1` to `WFB-23` under 310 CMR 10.55.
2. **Two Isolated Vegetated Wetland (IVW) Systems:** Protected under Town of Hingham Wetlands Protection By-Law Article 22.
3. **Confirmed Vernal Pool:** Located in the southwestern quadrant, creating strict 100-foot buffer and critical habitat setbacks.
4. **Perennial Stream & 200-Foot Riverfront Area:** Along Mean Annual High Water flags `BF1-1` to `BF1-36` and `BF1-100` to `BF1-123`.
