### PILLAR 1: WHERE WE ARE TODAY (CURRENT STATUS SNAPSHOT)

* **Fee Simple Ownership & Legal Title:** The Hingham Housing Authority (HHA) holds unencumbered fee simple title to **Lot B (~8.6 Acres)** at 100 Beal Street under the December 1989 Deed (*Plymouth County Registry of Deeds, Book 9516, Page 291*). The property is protected by a recorded **Notice of Statutory Transfer Restriction** (*Book 51379, Page 244*) pursuant to **M.G.L. c. 121B, § 34**, legally barring any municipal diversion without Massachusetts Executive Office of Housing and Livable Communities (EOHLC) approval.
* **Designated Developer & Ground Lease:** Peabody Properties, Inc. was designated as the private development partner following a competitive public RFP process. Development is structured under a **99-year ground lease** accompanied by a **\$500,000 upfront lease payment** directly to the HHA, ensuring permanent public land retention.
* **Fiscal Impact & Taxpayer Cost:** **\$0 Municipal Tax Dollars and \$0 Town Borrowing.** The project requires no town debt exclusion, capital outlay, or municipal operational funding. 100% of construction and operating costs are privately financed by Peabody Properties through Low-Income Housing Tax Credits (LIHTC), state subsidies, and private equity.
* **Permitting & Regulatory Track:** The development team is actively advancing its **MassHousing Project Eligibility Letter (PEL)** application, the statutory prerequisite for filing a **Comprehensive Permit under M.G.L. Chapter 40B** with the Hingham Zoning Board of Appeals (ZBA). Environmental boundaries are fully governed by a finalized, unappealed MassDEP Order of Resource Area Delineation.

#### Detailed Component Specifications:
1. **Site & Land Layout:** Development is strictly confined to the ~3.5-acre previously disturbed, level upland plateau on Lot B. Surrounding wooded topography and mature tree stands along Bare Cove Park will remain undisturbed natural visual buffers.
2. **Building Program & Amenities:** A single three-story, energy-efficient building comprising 68 one- and two-bedroom apartments tailored for independent seniors (ages 62 and older). Features universal design, full elevator accessibility, laundry suites, multi-purpose community rooms, and professional property management.
3. **Affordability & Preference:** 100% of all 68 units are restricted as affordable housing on the state Subsidized Housing Inventory (SHI). Units are tiered across Area Median Income (AMI) bands (30% to 80% AMI) to support fixed-income elders. In accordance with EOHLC guidelines, up to 70% of initial units will feature local preference for Hingham residents, veterans, and municipal employees.
4. **Financing & Zero Tax Impact:** Peabody Properties shoulders 100% of the financial liability and capital expenditure through federal/state Low-Income Housing Tax Credits (LIHTC), state affordable housing trust funds, and private construction lending. The Town of Hingham incurs zero debt and zero tax increases. The HHA receives \$500,000 in upfront lease revenue.
5. **Governance & Legal Title:** The HHA maintains fee ownership of the underlying land in perpetuity. The 99-year ground lease and Land Disposition & Development Agreement (LDDA) ensure long-term public oversight under M.G.L. c. 121B. The ministerial recording of the 2006 Town Selectmen vote permanently cures the unrecorded title technicality.
6. **Watershed & Environmental Protection:** Under MassDEP ORAD #034-1509, all development activities will sit outside the strict 100-foot wetland buffer. Depression D has been legally verified as non-jurisdictional upland. Closed-loop, state-of-the-art civil stormwater infrastructure ensures 100% on-site infiltration, safeguarding Tucker's Swamp and the Weymouth Back River watershed.
