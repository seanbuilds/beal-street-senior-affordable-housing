11. Motion to approve and authorize the Executive Director to execute a Change Proposal not to exceed $7,500.00
for project # 131098, Sprinkler Head Replacement, Beal St. This change order is to upgrade the sprinkler heads
to new Industrial type heads which are designed to withstand a shock. Original scope of project was to replace
existing non-concealed heads with Concealed heads to eliminate the possibility of the residents accidentally
breaking a sprinkler head and causing water damage to the building. Existing sprinkler heads are original and
are seized, hence cannot be removed with the industry standard tools without having to cut holes in the ceiling.
The industrial heads are special order and more expensive. The contractor is sending HHA a Change Order

Proposal but is waiting on a final quote from his supplier. The contractor believes that the cost is closer to
$6,000.00. This motion is being proposed to obtain board approval before the September Board meeting such
that we do not have to delay the start the project. The current Contract Cost of the project is $21,175.00.
