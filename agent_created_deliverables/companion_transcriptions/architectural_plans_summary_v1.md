## 3. Wetland Delineation & Environmental Filings Summary (`100-Beal-Street-ANRAD-Application.pdf`)

- **Environmental Consulting Scientist:** Christopher M. Lucas, PWS, Lucas Environmental, LLC
- **MassDEP File Number:** DEP No. 034-1509
- **Filing Date:** October 15, 2024
- **Field Inspection Dates:** November 2, 2023 and August 6, 2024; CPA field instrument location on August 16, 2024.
- **Delineated Resource Areas:**
  - **Bordering Vegetated Wetlands (BVW):** Flags `WFA-1` to `WFA-100` and `WFB-1` to `WFB-23` under 310 CMR 10.55 and Town of Hingham Wetlands Protection Bylaw (Article 22). Dominant vegetation includes red maple (*Acer rubrum*), highbush blueberry (*Vaccinium corymbosum*), winterberry (*Ilex verticillata*), and cinnamon fern (*Osmundastrum cinnamomeum*).
  - **Isolated Vegetated Wetlands (IVW):** Flagged as Wetland D (`WFD-1` to `WFD-6`). Protected under the local Hingham Wetlands Bylaw.
  - **Inland Bank / Perennial Stream & Riverfront Area (RFA):** Delineated along MAHW flags `BF1-1` through `BF1-36` and `BF1-100` through `BF1-123`. Establishes 100-foot inner riparian and 200-foot outer riparian Riverfront Areas under 310 CMR 10.58.
  - **Off-Site Wetland Overlaps:** 100-foot buffer zones extending onto the parcel from off-site BVW (`WFE-1` to `WFE-4`) and off-site IVW C (`WFC-1` to `WFC-6`).

## 4. Architectural & Civil Engineering Design (Weston & Sampson / Peabody)

Based on the ultra-high-resolution March 3, 2026 site plan update (`20260303-Beal-Street-Housing-Rendered-Plan-Update.jpg`) and architectural renderings:

- **Building Massing & Typology:**
  - 3-story modern residential building footprint specifically designed to step into the natural woodland topography.
  - Total unit capacity: 68 senior affordable rental apartments (advancing from earlier 50-unit and 60-unit conceptual iterations).
  - Dedicated common amenity spaces: central multi-purpose community hall, wellness suite, quiet reading rooms, administrative management office, and package reception.
- **Site Access & Circulation:**
  - Main vehicular ingress/egress from Beal Street through an improved, traffic-calmed curb cut.
  - Surface parking carefully arranged along the northern and interior facade, avoiding intrusion into the 50-foot wetland setback.
  - Full perimeter ADA-compliant paved multi-use walking loop connecting to outdoor recreation zones and Bare Cove Park paths.
- **Landscape Architecture & Stormwater:**
  - Low Impact Development (LID) bio-retention swales, rain gardens, and subterranean infiltration chambers engineered to protect the Weymouth Back River watershed.
  - Native pollinator gardens, sensory planting zones, shade trees to mitigate urban heat island effects, and a resident raised-bed garden patio.
  - Outdoor gathering terraces featuring shade pergolas and bench seating.

## 5. Summary of Regulatory & Permitting Trajectory

- **Early Feasibility (GM2 Associates / MHP):** Initial site assessment of 15 acres of HHA land; conceptualized 50 units with parking lot relocation.
- **RFP Award (August 2025):** Hingham Housing Authority Board of Commissioners formally voted to select Peabody Properties / AHSC as the designated developer.
- **Conservation Commission (Fall 2024 – 2026):** ANRAD filed under DEP File # 034-1509; Order of Resource Area Delineation (ORAD) issued confirming CPA / Lucas Environmental wetland boundary flagging.
- **Comprehensive Permitting (Chapter 40B):** Formal application pipeline for a friendly Comprehensive Permit under M.G.L. c. 40B with the Hingham Zoning Board of Appeals (ZBA) for 68 units of senior affordable housing.
