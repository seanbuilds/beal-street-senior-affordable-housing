# Municipal Meeting Records & Chronological Timeline: 100 Beal Street Senior Affordable Housing

<!-- v1 – Initial comprehensive timeline and meeting records index -->

This record compiles all public hearings, executive sessions, votes, and board deliberations concerning **100 Beal Street Senior Affordable Housing** (School Tract II) across the **Hingham Housing Authority (HHA)**, **Hingham Select Board**, **Conservation Commission**, **Affordable Housing Trust**, and **Zoning Board of Appeals (ZBA)**.
