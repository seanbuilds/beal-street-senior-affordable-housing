## 3. Authoritative Findings & Documentary Chain of Evidence

1. **The 1989 Conveyance & 30-Year Reverter (Book 09097, Pages 158–161):**
   - Executed March 7, 1989; recorded April 21, 1989 (Instrument #29758).
   - Town conveyed 15.014 acres (School Tract II) to HHA for $45,751.00.
   - Deed Page 3 (Page 160) restricted use to an adolescent facility and established a right of re-entry for condition broken, explicitly limited: *"provided, however, that said right of entry shall terminate after thirty years from the date hereof as provided in Massachusetts General Laws, Chapter 184A, Section 3."*

2. **Statutory Expiration of Reverter (March 7, 2019):**
   - Under **M.G.L. c. 184A, § 3** (recodified c. 184A, § 7) and **M.G.L. c. 184, § 23**, thirty years elapsed from March 7, 1989 without breach of condition (adolescent home continuously operated).
   - The Town's reversionary interest extinguished by operation of law on **March 7, 2019**, vesting unencumbered **fee simple absolute title in the Hingham Housing Authority**.

3. **Select Board Executive Session Litigation Vote (February 26, 2019):**
   - At 6:45 PM on February 26, 2019—just nine days before the statutory 30-year expiration—the Board of Selectmen (Paul Healey, Mary Power, Karen Johnson) met in Executive Session with Town Real Estate Counsel Susan Murphy.
   - Selectmen Johnson and Healey cited taxpayer funds expended under the 2001 Memorandum of Understanding (Plan 100 of 2001) and declared an impasse.
   - **Official Vote:** *Mr. Healey moved to file litigation regarding enforcement of agreement as to 100 Beal Street. Seconded by Ms. Johnson. Passed unanimously 3–0.*

4. **HHA Capitulation Under Lawsuit Threat (June 5, 2019):**
   - Confronted by the Town's litigation threat, the previous HHA Board of Commissioners voted at a Special Meeting on June 5, 2019 to transfer approximately nineteen (19) acres back to the Town.

5. **Commonwealth Statutory Veto (DHCD Letters & Book 51379, Page 244):**
   - The Massachusetts Department of Housing and Community Development (DHCD / EOHLC), via Associate Director Amy Stitely, intervened via formal warning letters dated April 19, 2019 and June 26, 2019.
   - **Statutory Authority:** Under **M.G.L. c. 121B, § 3**, local housing authorities are independent public bodies corporate, not town departments. Under **M.G.L. c. 121B, § 34**, property developed with state public housing funds (Chapter 689, Project 689-01) cannot be transferred or alienated without written approval from the DHCD Director and Attorney General review.
   - DHCD ruled the June 5, 2019 HHA transfer motion legally void and *ultra vires*.
   - On **July 18, 2019 at 1:44 PM**, DHCD recorded a formal **Notice of Statutory Transfer Restriction** at **Book 51379, Page 244** (Doc #55967), legally clouding and encumbering the title against municipal conveyance.

6. **The Rescission Vote (September 7, 2021):**
   - Under new Executive Director James Marathas, after direct consultations between DHCD legal counsel and Town Counsel, the HHA Board of Commissioners voted **4–0** (Motion by Commissioner O'Meara, second by Commissioner Lauter) to **formally rescind the June 5, 2019 transfer motion**, confirming that 100 Beal Street remains in the ownership of the HHA.

7. **Subsequent Disposition & Permitting (2024–2026):**
   - **Feb 20, 2024:** HHA Board declares Lot B surplus under M.G.L. c. 30B, § 16.
   - **Dec 10, 2024:** Order of Resource Area Delineation (ORAD, MassDEP File # 034-1509) recorded at Book 59527, Page 273.
   - **Apr 16, 2025:** HHA issues 155-page Developer RFP for 8.6-acre Lot B (triple-net 99-year ground lease).
   - **Aug 12, 2025:** HHA votes 4–0 to award developer designation to Peabody Properties / Affordable Housing Scenario Consultants (AHSC).
   - **Aug 25, 2026:** HHA Board approves Land Disposition Agreement (LDA) with AHSC Peabody Developers LLC.
