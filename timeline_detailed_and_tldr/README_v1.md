## Quick Summary of Key Milestones

* **1989:** Town deeded 15 acres of former Navy depot land to the Hingham Housing Authority for $45,751 for adolescent group care with a 30-year reverter (Book 09097, Page 158).
* **2001:** WWII munitions bunkers demolished by Town; survey divided property into 2-acre youth home and 8-acre surplus plateau (Plan Book 44, Page 412).
* **2006:** Town Meeting voted by declared 2/3 majority to allow senior affordable housing on the land (Warrant Article 38).
* **2019:** Select Board voted 3–0 to sue HHA to take the land back 9 days before the 30-year deed mark. State DHCD intervened under M.G.L. c. 121B, § 34, recorded a transfer restriction at Book 51379, Page 244, and blocked the transfer.
* **2021:** Housing Authority voted 4–0 to rescind the 2019 transfer, confirming the land remains with HHA.
* **2024:** Conservation Commission approved Order of Resource Area Delineation (ORAD File No. 034-1509; Book 59527, Page 273), stamping 155+ wetland flags protecting Tucker's Swamp.
* **2025:** HHA issued 155-page procurement RFP and voted 4–0 to award the 68-unit development to Peabody Properties under a 99-year ground lease with $500k upfront fee.
* **2026:** Town Meeting defeated Warrant Article 12 ($29.93M borrowing for Center for Active Living in Bare Cove Park). 100 Beal Street was never voted on and remains active.
