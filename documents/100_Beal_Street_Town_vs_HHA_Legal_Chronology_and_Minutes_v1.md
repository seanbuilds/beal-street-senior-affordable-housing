## 1. Statutory Foundations & The 1989 Fee Conveyance

### 1.1 The Original Fee Deed (Book 09097, Pages 158–161)
* **Date of Execution:** March 7, 1989  
* **Date of Recording:** April 21, 1989 at 11:58 AM  
* **Recording Citation:** Plymouth County Registry of Deeds, **Book 09097, Page 158** (Instrument #29758; 4 pages total, pp. 158–161)  
* **Grantor:** Town of Hingham, acting by and through its Board of Selectmen (Alexander A. Randall, Chairman; John P. Ryan; William J. Salisbury)  
* **Grantee:** Hingham Housing Authority (HHA)  
* **Consideration:** $45,751.00 paid into the Town Treasury  
* **Legal Description:** School Tract II, containing **15.014 net acres**, as depicted on Perkins Engineering Plan recorded in Plan Book 17, Page 508.  

#### Verbatim Deed Condition & 30-Year Reverter Clause (Book 09097, Page 160)
The deed conveyed fee simple title subject to an express educational restriction and an automatic 30-year limitation:

> *"This conveyance is made upon the express condition that the property will be used only for a residential educational facility for emotionally disturbed adolescents operated through the Hingham Housing Authority by the South Shore Educational Collaborative or similar entity...*  
>  
> *In the event that a breach of the above condition should occur after December 17, 2003, all right, title and interest in and to the above described property shall revert to and become the property of the Town of Hingham which shall have the immediate right of entry thereon, **provided, however, that said right of entry shall terminate after thirty years from the date hereof as provided in Massachusetts General Laws, Chapter 184A, Section 3.**"*

### 1.2 The Statutory Expiration Mechanics: March 7, 2019
Under Massachusetts General Laws governing property restrictions:
* **M.G.L. c. 184A, § 3** (recodified as M.G.L. c. 184A, § 7 by St. 1989, c. 668) and **M.G.L. c. 184, § 23**: A fee simple determinable or fee simple subject to a right of entry for condition broken becomes a **fee simple absolute** if the specified contingency does not occur within thirty years from the date when the interest becomes possessory.
* **Statutory Calculation:** Deed executed on **March 7, 1989**.  
  $$	ext{Deed Execution: March 7, 1989} + 30 	ext{ Years} = \mathbf{	ext{Statutory Expiration: March 7, 2019}}$$
* **The Reality:** The property was continuously utilized as an adolescent residential facility (under contract with the Department of Children and Families [DCF] and operated by Bay State Community Services). Because no breach occurred within the 30-year window, **the Town's possibility of reverter and right of re-entry statutorily extinguished by operation of law on March 7, 2019**.

## 2. The 2001 MOU & 2006 Town Meeting Authorization

### 2.1 The Memorandum of Understanding (June 12, 2001)
* **Parties:** Town of Hingham (Board of Selectmen) and Hingham Housing Authority.
* **Subject Matter:** Site remediation and environmental testing of former naval ammunition depot bunkers; potential future re-subdivision of School Tract II.
* **Survey Plan of Record:** *"Division of School Tract II, Beal Street, Hingham, MA"*, prepared by Gale Associates, Inc., dated June 8, 2001, recorded at the Plymouth County Registry of Deeds as **Plan 100 of 2001**.
* **Delineation:** Carved out a 2.0-acre developed building lot (adolescent home) and an 8.02-acre surplus undeveloped parcel (Lot 2).

### 2.2 2006 Annual Town Meeting Action (Article 38)
* **Meeting Date:** April 24, 2006  
* **Warrant Article 38:**
  > *"ARTICLE 38. Will the Town authorize and direct the Town of Hingham, acting through its Board of Selectmen, to release, revise and/or amend that certain restriction contained in that certain deed from the Town of Hingham to the Hingham Housing Authority dated March 7, 1989, recorded in the Plymouth County Registry of Deeds in Book 9097, Page 158, conveying School Tract II (15.014 acres), or act on anything related thereto?"*
* **Town Meeting Vote:** Voted by unanimous voice vote in favor of Article 38 to allow affordable housing qualifying for the state Subsidized Housing Inventory (SHI).
* **Registry Census Finding:** Although Town Meeting authorized the Board of Selectmen to execute an amendment, **a complete audit of the Plymouth County Registry of Deeds under Hingham Housing Authority confirms that the Select Board never executed or recorded a deed amendment or certificate of release**. The 1989 deed remained legally unamended on the record.

## 4. The HHA Capitulation & Commonwealth Statutory Veto

### 4.1 The HHA Special Meeting Vote (June 5, 2019)
Under the immediate threat of active litigation from Town Counsel, the Hingham Housing Authority Board of Commissioners held a Special Meeting on **June 5, 2019**:
* Under legal duress, the then-Board of Commissioners passed a motion to capitulate to the Town's demands.
* **The Motion:** Approved transferring approximately nineteen (19) acres (School Tract II) back to the Town of Hingham.

### 4.2 Commonwealth Administrative Intervention: DHCD Warning Letters
Upon learning of the proposed transfer, the **Massachusetts Department of Housing and Community Development (DHCD)**—the state supervisory body under the Executive Office of Housing and Livable Communities (EOHLC)—intervened:
* **State Authorities of Record:** Amy Stitely, Associate Director of Public Housing; DHCD Chief Legal Counsel.
* **Official State Letters:** Formal warning letters issued to HHA and Town Counsel on **April 19, 2019** and **June 26, 2019**.
* **Statutory Determination:** 
  1. Under **M.G.L. c. 121B, § 3**, local housing authorities are independent public bodies corporate and politic created by the General Court, not subordinate municipal departments.
  2. Because the adolescent facility at 100 Beal Street was developed using state public housing funding (**Chapter 689 State-Aided Public Housing Program, Project 689-01**), the real property is subject to **M.G.L. c. 121B, § 34**.
  3. Under M.G.L. c. 121B, § 34, **no local housing authority has the legal capacity to sell, convey, transfer, or alienate state-assisted public housing property without express written approval from the DHCD Director/Secretary and review by the Massachusetts Attorney General**.
* **The State's Ruling:** DHCD formally denied approval for any conveyance to the Town, declaring that the HHA's June 5, 2019 motion was *ultra vires*, legally void, and unenforceable under state law.

### 4.3 The State Title Encumbrance Recorded (July 18, 2019)
To legally enforce its determination on the real property records, the Commonwealth recorded a statutory notice at the registry:

* **Recording Date:** July 18, 2019 at 1:44 PM  
* **Recording Citation:** Plymouth County Registry of Deeds, **Book 51379, Page 244** (Document #55967; 5 pages total, pp. 244–248)  
* **Instrument Type:** Notice of Statutory Transfer Restriction (`NOTC`)  
* **Parties:** Massachusetts Department of Housing and Community Development (DHCD) & Hingham Housing Authority  
* **Subject Property:** 100 Beal Street, Lot 2, Hingham, MA  
* **Legal Effect:** Placed an active, recorded statutory encumbrance upon the title, providing public legal notice that the property cannot be transferred or conveyed without the express written consent of the Commonwealth.

## 5. The Definitive Rescission Vote: HHA Minutes (September 7, 2021)

Following the state's intervention, new executive leadership took charge of the HHA under Executive Director **James Marathas**. After consultation between DHCD legal counsel and Town Counsel, the HHA formally extinguished the 2019 transfer motion.

* **Location:** 30 Thaxter Street, Hingham, MA

* **Commissioners Present:** Gregory O’Meara, Irma Lauter, Megan Buhr, Janine Suchecki.

## 6. Official Procurement, Developer Award & Permitting Records (2024–2026)

With its fee simple ownership confirmed, the HHA initiated formal disposition and development under state procurement statutes:

* **HHA Board Meeting (February 20, 2024):** The Board of Commissioners voted unanimously to declare 100 Beal Street surplus to its immediate operational needs, authorize the preparation of a Request for Proposals (RFP) pursuant to **M.G.L. c. 30B, § 16**, and execute a technical assistance agreement with the Massachusetts Housing Partnership (MHP).
* *(Meeting Date Clarification: Rescheduled from February 13, 2024 due to winter storm).*

### 6.2 Authorization of Developer RFP (September 10, 2024)
* **HHA Board Meeting (September 10, 2024):** Board unanimously voted Motions 7 and 8 authorizing Executive Director James Marathas to finalize and issue the Developer RFP for affordable senior rental housing.

### 6.3 Issuance of Official Developer RFP (April 16, 2025)
* **Issuing Body:** Hingham Housing Authority  
* **RFP Document:** 155-page comprehensive solicitation package:
  * **Attachment A:** Civil Engineering Due Diligence & Feasibility Study (Bohler Engineering)
