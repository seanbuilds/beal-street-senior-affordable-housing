# Beal Street Senior Affordable Housing (100 Beal Street, Hingham, MA)
## Comprehensive Municipal Archive, Architectural Plans, Legal Records, Broadcasts & Media Repository

<!-- v2 – Expanded master archive following secondary exhaustive multi-agent deep search -->

This archive contains all town documents, architectural diagrams, engineering surveys, news articles, meeting minutes, dates/times, legal contracts, developer procurement files, and video broadcast references for the **Beal Street Senior Affordable Housing** project (also referenced as **100 Beal Street** or **School Tract II**) in Hingham, Massachusetts.

   - `Bohler_Due_Diligence_Feasibility_Report_and_Lucas_Wetlands.pdf` (9.8 MB): Standalone civil engineering due diligence feasibility study.
   - `Hingham_Town_Meeting_Warrant_2006_Article_38_School_Tract_II.pdf` & `Vote`: 2006 Town Meeting Warrant and vote amending the 1989 deed restriction to permit affordable senior housing on the Subsidized Housing Inventory (SHI).

   - `SRP2020-South-Shore-Site-Readiness.pdf`: MassDevelopment regional site readiness study for Beal Street.
